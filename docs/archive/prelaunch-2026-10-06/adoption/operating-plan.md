# A bounded adoption experiment and sustainability decision

Prepared 5 October 2026; all time allocations below are proposals, not observed maintainer hours. The recruitment clock starts after specifically authorized contact begins. Outreach is still held; the packet is ready for review. Independent use, repeat use and payment remain unproven.

## Four weeks, up to three maintainer hours each

| Week | Time allocation | Concrete result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 45 min review demo/article; 45 min review one launch post and at most two tailored invitations; 30 min approve/apply selected external actions; 60 min answer actual questions | One coherent public entry path and a small, accurately recorded invitation set. No automatic blast to every candidate. |
| 2 | 90 min observe up to two willing first attempts; 30 min classify obstacles; 60 min reproduce one recurring blocker | Record unaided behavior before helping, setup separately, assistance and abandonment. Reproduce the chosen blocker; do not promise a fix within an arbitrary deadline. |
| 3 | 60 min support one retained flow/CI; 60 min review a narrow fix if justified; 30 min prepare a factual learning; 30 min respond | A valuable test retained by its owner or one explained reason for rejection. Publication of a case study requires exact permission. |
| 4 | 60 min review actual separate later use; 60 min compare value and support burden; 30 min choose disposition; 30 min update the evidence | Continue modestly, revise one named onboarding/positioning problem, or end active promotion and maintain at a low cadence. |

If no one volunteers, spend the observation time reviewing the ask and public entry path rather than inventing interviews or adding features. No responses after the timebox are a weak signal about this approach, not proof that the underlying tool has no value. Do not continuously expand the audience or roadmap to chase a success count.

Provisional experiment signals: two independent useful first tests and one voluntary later use in a separate session. Installation, completed interaction, diagnosis, test retention and CI are separate observations. These smaller signals do not mark formal A1 complete: its participant-reviewed workflows, stacks, CI and retention thresholds remain in the existing plan. Impressions, stars, compliments and invitations are not adoption.

Keep one useful implementation change active at a time. Fix a reduced correctness failure first; otherwise pick the obstacle multiple participants actually experienced. Stop the promotional experiment at four weeks unless a concrete finding justifies another finite trial. Do not schedule automatic messages. A declined invitation gets no further contact.

## The first monetization decision

Keep the standalone runner useful and open-source without an account. The initial objective is reputation and independently useful tests. There is no revenue forecast or market-size estimate to support here.

Optional GitHub Sponsors is a possible low-obligation way for people to support maintenance. GitHub currently requires contributors to be in a supported region and complete its account/payout setup; personal eligibility has not been checked. One-time and monthly sponsorship options exist. A future page could simply thank supporters without promising prioritized fixes, an SLA or a newsletter. No account, funding file, tier or payment link has been activated. [Platform eligibility](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors-for-open-source-contributors) and [setup requirements](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/setting-up-github-sponsors-for-your-personal-account) were reviewed on 5 October.

If repeated independent use opens the existing commercial-discovery gate and the maintainer wants paid work, a scoped engagement is more appropriate than a service build. Example offer for discussion: one trusted project, one supported keyboard flow, pass/seeded defect/recovery, reviewed baseline, one participant-owned CI integration and a short handoff. Cap delivery at six support hours over two weeks after prerequisites are agreed. Exclude production credentials, general Unicode compatibility, custom hosting, unlimited revisions and ongoing availability. Before agreeing, identify the buyer, acceptance criteria, calendar, data handling and actual price. The hour cap is an offer hypothesis; it is not a delivery estimate or a customer commitment.

A PR comment alone has an existing substitute: a failed Actions check, summary and downloadable offline report. The new source workflow addresses that handoff without operating a backend. A hosted service should be reconsidered only if multiple repeat users identify a costly recurring workflow that this approach does not solve, and one approved paid pilot funds a concrete outcome. The existing A1/A2 gates still apply. Installation counts or an enthusiastic request do not justify arbitrary target execution, secrets handling, billing or tenancy work.

Current commercial disposition: preparation complete; no hosted build or substantive paid discovery started. Read [A2](../plans/release/08-commercial-discovery.md) for buyer/value evidence and the hosted-history decision criteria.

Agent-assisted implementation and drafting are allowed within this project, but the maintainer must verify claims and own the conversation; do not delegate unsupervised outreach.

The read-only invitation reconciliation found one explicit refusal among the earlier five invitations. Its identity and text are retained privately, with a no-contact marker. No consenting trial or adoption outcome follows from that comment. A small human-reviewed invitation set is more appropriate than repeated template posting.

Therefore this AI-written package is not a ready-to-post HN submission.
